Vendor+ · Event Organizer

Get approved to sell event tickets and spaces

Anyone can submit an event for calendar review. Separate approval is required before a Vendor+ member can sell admission tickets or reserve paid tables and booths through PokePrizePool.

What to upload
1. A clear photo of the front of your government-issued ID
2. A clear photo of the back of that ID
3. A clear selfie of you holding no ID is needed; use a recent, well-lit selfie showing your face clearly.

These images are used only for organizer verification. They are stored in a private bucket, are not public event media, and are deleted after an admin records a decision.
Checking account and verification status…

Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF images, maximum 8 MB each. Keep the whole ID visible and make sure text and photo are readable. Do not upload unrelated documents.

Your identity documents are not the same as Stripe’s separate payment onboarding. If approved, you must still complete Stripe setup before checkout can accept payments.
