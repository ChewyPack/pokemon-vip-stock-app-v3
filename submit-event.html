Submit an Event

Know about a Pokémon card show, trade night, tournament, or store event? Send it to PokePrizePool for review. Submissions are not published automatically; an admin checks the details before adding an approved event to the calendar.

Each occurrence up to this date will be listed separately after approval. Weekly series are limited to 53 dates, monthly to 60, and yearly to 10.

Please submit events you believe are accurate. We may contact you or check the official organizer listing before approving. Do not submit private personal information.

Once approved, the event will appear on the public Events Calendar. PokePrizePool may edit or decline submissions that are incomplete, unverifiable, expired, or duplicate entries.
