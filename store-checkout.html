How to complete checkout

1. Send payment

Use the current payment account or handle PokePrizePool has provided for your order. Accepted methods are:

VenmoPayPalZelleCash App

Send only the amount confirmed for your order. Do not estimate shipping or taxes from the listed-price subtotal. If you do not have the current verified handle or confirmed amount, contact PokePrizePool before paying.

2. Put your PokePrizePool username in the payment note/memo

In the payment note/memo, enter the same username you use on PokePrizePool so we can match your payment to your order.

3. Email your order request

Click Email Order Request below. The website will automatically send your items, quantities, variants, estimated subtotal, username, and preferred payment method to pokeprizepool@gmail.com. You do not need to open your own email app.
