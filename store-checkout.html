How to complete checkout

1. Send payment

Send the confirmed order amount using one of these PokePrizePool payment handles:

Include your PokePrizePool username in the payment note/memo. If the page labels the tax line as an estimate, confirm your final payable amount with PokePrizePool before sending money.

2. Put your PokePrizePool username in the payment note/memo

In the payment note/memo, enter the same username you use on PokePrizePool so we can match your payment to your order.

3. Email your order request

Click Email Order Request below. The website will automatically send your items, quantities, variants, estimated subtotal, username, and preferred payment method to pokeprizepool@gmail.com. You do not need to open your own email app.

Important: The page calculates the Rhode Island 7% tax estimate on the full product subtotal, then adds it to the subtotal. If the tax line says estimate only, PokePrizePool has not enabled tax collection in checkout yet, so confirm the amount before paying. Clothing/apparel is shipped sealed in packaging; singles, sealed Pokémon products, graded cards, and accessories are pickup-only for now. Mixed carts need separate fulfillment confirmation.
