How to complete checkout

Use the verified PokePrizePool payment account or handle provided by PokePrizePool. Do not guess or send money to an unverified recipient.

2. Put your PokePrizePool username in the payment note/memo

Enter the same username you use on PokePrizePool in the payment note or memo so we can match the payment to your order.

3. Email your order request

Click Email Order Request below. Your email app will open a draft addressed to pokeprizepool@gmail.com with the items, quantities, variants, estimated subtotal, username, and selected payment method. Review it and press Send.

Important: Wait until PokePrizePool confirms availability, the final total, and payment details before sending payment. The subtotal shown here is not a final invoice.
