Pricing at a glance

VIP rates lower the cost of your time. They do not promise priority booking. High-demand pricing may apply to major releases, such as premium collection boxes and UPCs, or challenging conditions such as snow and freezing temperatures. The applicable rate is confirmed before you pay.

Before you book, let's make sure it makes sense.

This service isn't for everyone, and that's completely understandable. High-demand Pokémon releases can require 7–10 hours of waiting, sometimes outdoors in freezing temperatures. You're paying for the time, effort, planning, and commitment required to make the purchasing attempt.

Best suited for collectors who want to maximize their available store purchase limits, subject to the retailer's rules:

  • Multiple eligible products
  • Premium boxes and high-demand releases
  • Full 2-per-SKU allowances where the store permits them

If you're looking for just one or two lower-priced items, the service fee may outweigh the value of the purchase. Use the calculator to decide whether it makes financial sense for you. Never spend beyond your budget.

Payment rules

You pay two separate amounts: the service fee and your product budget.

1. Service fee: paid upfront

  • Paid in advance to secure the reservation.
  • Every hourly booking has a 3-hour minimum.
  • A reservation isn't confirmed until availability is confirmed and payment requirements are satisfied.

2. Product funds: before opening

  • The full budget for the requested products must be provided before the store opens.
  • Product funds are separate from the service fee.
  • No product funds means no purchase attempt.

Refund, cancellation, and reimbursement terms have not been defined here and must be confirmed directly before you pay.
