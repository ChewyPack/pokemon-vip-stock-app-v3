Sealed product inspection

Applies to booster packs, booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes, collection boxes, tins, and other sealed products.

Packaging

Compare artwork, logos, text, and product identifiers with official product images for the same product and region.

Wrapping and seals

Look for unusual construction, glue residue, or signs of tampering. Wrapping can differ across regions, releases, and production runs, so no single seal or logo proves authenticity.

The listing

Confirm the listing describes the actual product being sold, with real photographs of the item itself.

Seller and price

Check purchase history, reputation, and return policy. A price or claim that seems too good deserves further investigation.

Counterfeit vs. opened or resealed

Counterfeit packaging means the product itself is not genuine. Opened or resealed means a potentially genuine product may have had its contents removed or swapped. These are separate concerns. Evaluate each on its own evidence, and do not cut or destroy a sealed product to test it.

Common myths and dangerous tests

Do not do these. They can destroy a card's value and none of them is conclusive:

  • Ripping a card to look for internal layers.
  • Bending a card to test flexibility.
  • Scratching the surface or scraping off foil.
  • Cutting or destroying a sealed product.
  • Treating a flashlight test as definitive proof.
  • Relying on weight, texture, or any single visual clue.

No single informal test authenticates every Pokémon card. Instead, compare side by side with a known authentic example, check reputable card databases, and get an experienced second opinion on expensive purchases.

Before you buy

  • Request clear photos of the front, back, packaging, and relevant identifiers.
  • Compare against official product information or a reputable card database.
  • Check the seller's history and any applicable buyer protection.
  • Review return and dispute policies before paying.
  • Avoid irreversible payment methods with unfamiliar sellers.
  • For expensive cards, consider an independent professional authentication service.
  • Walk away if material inconsistencies remain unresolved.

If you suspect a fake

  1. Pause the purchase or stop further transactions.
  2. Preserve receipts, order confirmations, listing screenshots, messages, and photographs.
  3. Contact the seller or marketplace through its official dispute process.
  4. Contact Pokémon Support when appropriate, particularly for suspected counterfeit products or possible manufacturing issues.
  5. For a high-value collectible, seek a qualified independent opinion before making irreversible decisions.

Pokémon Support distinguishes counterfeit concerns from eligible manufacturing defects, and its assistance and exchange policies may have specific requirements. No refund, replacement, or outcome is guaranteed.
