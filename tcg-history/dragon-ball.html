The story behind the battles

Dragon Ball Trading Card Game History

Dragon Ball has inspired generations of card collectors and competitive players. Across different card-game editions, fans have collected their favorite warriors, built decks, and recreated the series’ high-energy battles at the table.

1984

The Dragon Ball story begins

Akira Toriyama’s Dragon Ball manga began publication in 1984, introducing Goku and a world of martial arts, adventure, and powerful rivals that would become a global pop-culture phenomenon.

2000s

Early collectible card games

Dragon Ball received multiple collectible and trading card releases over the years. Different editions brought character cards, battle-focused mechanics, and artwork inspired by the anime and manga to collectors and players.

2017

Dragon Ball Super Card Game

The Dragon Ball Super Card Game launched with leaders, energy management, combos, and battles built around characters from across the Dragon Ball universe. It developed a dedicated community of players and collectors.

2024

A new era with Fusion World

Dragon Ball Super Card Game Fusion World introduced a streamlined ruleset and a new way to play, alongside digital play and physical releases, broadening the ways fans could experience Dragon Ball cards.

Today

Collectors and competitors

Dragon Ball card games continue to attract players who enjoy strategic matches as well as collectors drawn to character art, special treatments, and cards featuring favorite moments from the franchise.
