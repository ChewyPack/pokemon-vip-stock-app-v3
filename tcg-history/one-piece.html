The story behind the cards

One Piece Card Game History

Inspired by Eiichiro Oda’s world of pirates, crews, and adventure, the One Piece Card Game brings the series to the tabletop through character-led decks, strategic battles, and collectible artwork.

Back to the TCG showcase Report a sighting
1997

The manga begins

One Piece began serialization in Japan in 1997. The long-running story of Monkey D. Luffy and the Straw Hat crew would eventually inspire a global community of fans, artists, and collectors.

2022

The card game is announced

Bandai announced the One Piece Card Game as a new way to experience the series through collectible cards and head-to-head play. Character cards, Leader cards, DON!! cards, and resource management became central parts of its identity.

2022

First releases and organized play

The game launched in Japan in July 2022, followed by its English-language release later that year. Starter decks made it easier to begin playing, while booster sets expanded deck-building choices and collecting goals.

2023

A growing competitive scene

Local tournaments, store events, and larger competitions helped players test new strategies and build community. Leaders, colors, archetypes, and evolving set releases gave the game a fast-moving metagame.

Today

Play, collect, and chase favorites

The One Piece TCG now appeals to competitive players, fans collecting favorite characters, and collectors drawn to alternate-art cards and special rarities. New releases continue to add characters and moments from across the One Piece world.
