Collector watchlist

Signals to investigate — not automatic buy or sell calls.

What to keep an eye on

  • Whether a price move persists across multiple daily snapshots.
  • Whether market, low, and mid prices move in the same direction.
  • PSA population growth and the pace of new graded supply.
  • Restock reports with timestamps, location, product counts, and firsthand evidence.

What to consider selling

We won’t label a card “sell now” from percentage movement alone. Before making a decision, compare recent completed sales, your cost basis, fees, shipping, condition, and how quickly the item is actually selling.

Market data is informational, not a guarantee of future prices or sale proceeds.

Data transparency: Pokémon Predictor states that its price snapshots come from TCGplayer aggregate market data through tcgcsv, population information is tracked from PSA data by GemRate, and graded price references come from PriceCharting where labeled.
