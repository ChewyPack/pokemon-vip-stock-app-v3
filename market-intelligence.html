A card hype tracker can combine grading submissions, submission growth relative to the graded base, seven-day price movement, and total graded supply. Treat rapid attention as a prompt to investigate liquidity and supply, not proof of sustainable demand.

Cheap SIR / hyper-rare research

A useful research board can cover English SIR, Hyper Rare, and Mega Hyper Rare cards across Scarlet & Violet and Mega-era sets, using graded-price references where available. Confirm current prices and missing values before comparing cards.

Promos & regional exclusives

Promo galleries use set/population identification and price references; regional-exclusive coverage is described as verified releases with market presence, not a guaranteed exhaustive checklist. Confirm release details before treating an item as region-exclusive.

Market data quality checks

The supplied verified-movers description says market, low, and mid must move in the same direction, the row must have refreshed recently, and market price cannot sit below its own listing floor. The methodology page explains the precise filters and why they exist. These checks reduce obvious anomalies but do not eliminate all risk.

Collector watchlist & field intelligence

Signals to investigate — not automatic buy or sell calls.

What to monitor

  • Whether price movement persists across multiple daily snapshots.
  • Recent completed sales, sales volume, spread, and listing depth—not just the lowest ask.
  • PSA population growth and the pace of new graded supply.
  • Artist / card attention alongside actual price and liquidity.
  • Set release calendars, promo distribution, regional exclusives, and restock patterns.
  • PokePrizePool firsthand reports with timestamps, store location, quantities, and photos where available.

Before selling or buying

We will not label a card “buy now” or “sell now” from a percentage move alone. Compare recent completed sales, cost basis, fees, shipping, condition, and actual sell-through. High percentage moves on thinly traded cards can reverse quickly.

Market data is informational, not a guarantee of future prices or sale proceeds.
