More research signals

Coverage beyond a movers leaderboard.

Grading ROI calculator

Estimate outcomes using grading fees, selling costs, expected grade distribution, and grade-specific resale values. Population-based PSA 10 odds can overstate the chance of a random raw copy receiving a 10; use assumptions conservatively.

Artist spotlight & hype meter

Predictor describes an artist spotlight and a descriptive hype meter combining grading velocity (35%), card value (25%), price heat (20%), and forum chatter (20%). It is a sentiment/attention indicator, not a forecast or guaranteed signal.

Card hype tracker

Predictor’s stated mix uses grading submissions (45%), submission growth relative to base (20%), seven-day price movement (25%), and graded base (10%). Treat rapid attention as a prompt to investigate liquidity and supply, not proof of sustainable demand.

Cheap SIR / hyper-rare research

Predictor describes a board covering English SIR, Hyper Rare, and Mega Hyper Rare cards across Scarlet & Violet and Mega-era sets, using PriceCharting values and excluding cards without PSA 10 prices. Check the current board for its full list.

Promos & regional exclusives

Promo galleries use set/population identification and price references; regional-exclusive coverage is described as verified releases with market presence, not a guaranteed exhaustive checklist. Confirm release details before treating an item as region-exclusive.

Market data quality checks

Predictor says it screens out stale rows, requires market / low / mid values to move in the same direction for movers, and excludes cases where market price is less than 70% of listing low. These filters reduce obvious anomalies but do not eliminate all risk.

Collector watchlist & field intelligence

Signals to investigate — not automatic buy or sell calls.

What to monitor

  • Whether price movement persists across multiple daily snapshots.
  • Recent completed sales, sales volume, spread, and listing depth—not just the lowest ask.
  • PSA population growth and the pace of new graded supply.
  • Artist / card attention alongside actual price and liquidity.
  • Set release calendars, promo distribution, regional exclusives, and restock patterns.
  • PokePrizePool firsthand reports with timestamps, store location, quantities, and photos where available.

Before selling or buying

We will not label a card “buy now” or “sell now” from a percentage move alone. Compare recent completed sales, cost basis, fees, shipping, condition, and actual sell-through. High percentage moves on thinly traded cards can reverse quickly.

Market data is informational, not a guarantee of future prices or sale proceeds.
